## Support Categories

| Category | Target response | Owner | Notes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Login/account access | 2 business days | Support | Verify identity before changes. |
| Billing/refund/cancel | 2 business days | Billing | Follow published policy exactly. |
| Product setup | 3 business days | Support | Local environments vary; no guaranteed setup success unless plan says so. |
| AI output/code issue | 3 business days | Support | Remind user of review/testing responsibility. |
| Recording/privacy concern | 1 business day | Privacy | Escalate if personal data or third-party complaint. |
| Security report | 5 business days acknowledgement | Security | Use vulnerability disclosure policy. |
| Chargeback/dispute | ASAP | Billing | Preserve evidence and Terms acceptance. |
| Abuse report | 2 business days | Support/Legal | Apply AUP. |

## Refund Handling

1. Check published refund policy version in effect at purchase.
2. Check purchase date and plan.
3. Check whether physical merch was involved.
4. Document reason.
5. Approve or deny consistently.
6. If approved, process through Stripe.
7. Confirm cancellation status separately from refund.
8. Log outcome.

## Chargeback Handling

Collect:

1. Customer email.
2. Stripe customer/subscription/invoice IDs.
3. Purchase timestamp.
4. Terms/refund policy version.
5. Checkout page or pricing copy in effect.
6. Usage/access records.
7. Support/refund correspondence.
8. Cancellation status.
9. Fulfillment proof if merch.

Respond factually. Do not attack the customer. Resolve policy mismatches so they do not recur.

## AI Output Complaints

When a user reports bad AI output or generated code:

1. Acknowledge.
2. Ask for product, mode, provider, prompt, output, and impact.
3. Do not admit professional responsibility for the user's reliance.
4. Point to AI Output Policy.
5. Offer product support where appropriate.
6. Escalate security/privacy/legal if output exposed data, caused data loss, or implicated regulated advice.

## Recording/Capture Complaints

When a person claims they were recorded, transcribed, captured, or monitored:

1. Treat as privacy-sensitive.
2. Identify whether requester is user, bystander, employee, customer, or third party.
3. Preserve communications.
4. Do not disclose another user's account data without verification/legal basis.
5. Point to Recording/Capture Policy.
6. Escalate to legal/privacy review.

## Tone Rule

Be clear, calm, factual, and consistent. Never improvise legal promises in support replies.
