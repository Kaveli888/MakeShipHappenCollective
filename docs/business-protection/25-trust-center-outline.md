## Compliance Roadmap Should Not Say

Do not claim:

1. SOC 2 certified.
2. HIPAA compliant.
3. FERPA compliant.
4. GDPR compliant.
5. Enterprise certified.
6. Zero hallucination.
7. No telemetry.
8. Never leaves your machine.

Unless each is fully supported by controls, contracts, evidence, and attorney-approved language.

## Safer Compliance Language

"MakeShipHappen is building toward enterprise-grade controls. Current documentation includes Terms, Privacy, Acceptable Use, subprocessors, vulnerability disclosure, incident response, and product-specific data-flow documentation. Certifications and regulated-use agreements are not offered unless expressly stated in a signed agreement."
