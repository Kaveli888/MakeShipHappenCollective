# Community Rules

Status: Public-facing draft for Discord/community.

The MakeShipHappen community exists for builders learning, shipping, and using AI responsibly.

## Rules

1. Be respectful. No harassment, hate, threats, stalking, or targeted abuse.
2. Do not share private information, secrets, API keys, credentials, customer data, private code, or confidential material.
3. Do not request or provide help for malware, phishing, credential theft, unauthorized access, evasion, scraping abuse, spam, or illegal activity.
4. Do not post nonconsensual recordings, screenshots, transcripts, or personal data.
5. Do not present AI output as verified professional legal, medical, financial, or security advice.
6. Do not impersonate MakeShipHappen staff, partners, customers, or other members.
7. Do not spam, scrape, sell, recruit aggressively, or abuse DMs.
8. Respect intellectual property. Share only content you have the right to share.
9. Keep feedback constructive.
10. Follow platform rules, including Discord's terms and policies.

## Moderation

MakeShipHappen may remove content, warn users, restrict access, ban users, report unlawful activity, or preserve evidence where necessary to protect the community, users, third parties, or the company.

## No Official Advice

Community discussion is not professional advice, legal advice, security certification, or official support unless explicitly provided through an official support channel.

## Support Boundaries

Community channels may help with general questions. Account, billing, privacy, legal, and security matters should go to the official contact emails.
