## Nevada LLC Baseline

Nevada LLC formation normally involves:

- Articles of Organization.
- Registered agent with a Nevada street address.
- Initial List of Managers or Managing Members.
- Nevada State Business License.
- Annual List and business license renewal.

The Nevada Secretary of State LLC fee schedule reviewed lists:

| Item | Listed fee |
| --- | --- |
| Articles of Organization | $75 |
| Initial List of Managers or Members | $150 |
| Annual/Amended List of Managers or Members | $150 |
| Business License Fee | $200 |
| Late Fee for List of Managers or Members | $75 |

Operational implication:

- Nevada is not just a $75 filing.
- First-year state filings can include Articles, Initial List, and State Business License.
- Recurring annual compliance includes Annual List plus State Business License renewal.

## Clark County Licensing Baseline

Clark County states that if a business location is based in a particular city outside unincorporated Clark County but the business travels into the County to conduct business, a Clark County business license may be required in addition to a city license.

Clark County's step-by-step guide says:

- Verify the primary jurisdiction using the Clark County jurisdiction locator.
- Register the entity and obtain a Nevada State Business License.
- Register with the Nevada Department of Taxation.
- If using a fictitious firm name / DBA, file that DBA with the Clark County Clerk.
- General license applications can be submitted online.

Operational implication:

- A Nevada LLC does not automatically finish local licensing.
- Clark County or city licensing depends on the actual business location and activity.
- If MakeShipHappen uses only the LLC's legal name, a DBA may not be needed.
- If MakeShipHappen uses a public trade name that differs from the exact entity name, a DBA/FFN review is needed.

## California Pull-In Risk

California FTB states that all domestic and foreign LLCs doing business in California are required to register with the California Secretary of State.

California's LLC tax page defines doing business as actively engaging in any transaction for financial gain or profit and states the $800 annual LLC tax applies if the LLC is doing business in California and/or registered with the California Secretary of State.

California FTB Publication 3556 includes examples where a California resident/member of a Nevada LLC can cause California filing obligations by managing or conducting business from California.

California Secretary of State says a foreign business entity must qualify/register before transacting intrastate business in California, and that foreign LLC registration requires an out-of-state LLC registration with a valid certificate of good standing.

Operational implication:

- If Jake forms a Nevada LLC but runs MakeShipHappen from California, California registration/tax obligations may still apply.
- This can create a two-state compliance burden: Nevada formation/annual filings plus California foreign registration/tax filings.
- A Nevada ID alone is not the deciding factor; actual residence, business management, source income, operations, and physical presence matter.

## Decision Matrix

| Scenario | Better initial path | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jake lives and works primarily from Palm Springs, California | California LLC or California registration | Avoids pretending Nevada is the operating base when California may still apply. |
| Jake is actually Nevada-resident and operates from Clark County | Nevada LLC + Clark County/city licensing review | Nevada is the genuine operating state. |
| Jake has Nevada ID but currently operates from California | Attorney/CPA review before filing | High risk of dual-state filing/tax obligations. |
| Company expects customers nationwide but founder works from one state | Form/register where management actually occurs | Online customers do not erase founder operating nexus. |
| Future move back to Nevada is imminent | Ask CPA whether to wait, form Nevada now, or form CA then convert/register | Timing affects costs and filings. |
