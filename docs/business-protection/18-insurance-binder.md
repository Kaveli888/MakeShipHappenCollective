# Insurance Binder

Purpose: prepare underwriting materials for Cyber Liability, Technology Errors & Omissions, Media/IP, General Liability, and D&O.

## Recommended Insurance

| Coverage | Priority | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| Cyber Liability | Critical | Data breach, privacy claims, incident response, forensics, notification. |
| Technology Errors & Omissions | Critical | AI software, generated code, local agents, product failure, customer harm claims. |
| Media / IP Liability | Medium | Generated content, prompt libraries, marketing, copyright/trademark claims. |
| General Liability | Medium | Merch, events, physical business operations. |
| Directors & Officers | Later | Useful when raising capital, adding investors/advisors, or forming board. |
| Crime / Funds Transfer | Medium | Payment/vendor account risk, social engineering. |

## Underwriter Narrative

MakeShipHappen is an AI software company building local-first and cloud-optional tools for builders. Products process user-selected files, prompts, audio, transcripts, source code, and local app data. Payment card processing is handled by Stripe. Most sensitive user content is designed to be processed locally where configured, while optional cloud provider use is disclosed and governed by third-party provider terms. The company is building formal governance controls for privacy, acceptable use, AI output responsibility, incident response, vulnerability disclosure, and vendor management.

## Questions To Be Ready For

1. Do you store payment cards? Expected answer: no, Stripe handles card data.
2. Do you process PHI/FERPA data? Expected answer: not intentionally as a regulated provider unless future separate agreement exists.
3. Do you use AI providers? Yes, depending product and user configuration.
4. Do you allow user file/code access? Yes, for local desktop/developer tools under user control.
5. Do you record audio/screens? Some products support capture; user consent responsibility is documented.
6. Do you have incident response? Yes, see plan.
7. Do you have security contact? Yes, security@makeshiphappen.tech.
8. Do you have contracts/policies? Terms/Privacy/AUP in attorney review.
