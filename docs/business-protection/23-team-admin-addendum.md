# Team and Administrator Responsibility Addendum

Status: Public-facing draft for attorney review.

This addendum applies to Team, organization, workspace, community, or shared-account features.

## Administrator Responsibilities

Team administrators are responsible for:

1. Inviting only authorized users.
2. Removing users who no longer need access.
3. Assigning appropriate permissions.
4. Ensuring team members comply with company policy, law, and the Acceptable Use Policy.
5. Obtaining required consent for recording, transcription, screen capture, monitoring, or processing employee/customer data.
6. Managing organization-owned API keys, provider accounts, and billing.
7. Reviewing AI outputs and generated code before organizational use.
8. Maintaining backups and local device controls.
9. Handling internal employment, confidentiality, and access disputes.

## Team Member Responsibilities

Team members are responsible for:

1. Activity under their account.
2. Protecting credentials and API keys.
3. Using products only within authorized scopes.
4. Not uploading or processing unauthorized data.
5. Reviewing outputs before relying on them.
6. Complying with administrator policies.

## Organization Responsibility

The organization is responsible for determining whether MakeShipHappen products are appropriate for its regulatory, security, employment, privacy, and contractual obligations.

## Platform Responsibility

MakeShipHappen is responsible for providing the service according to applicable Terms, Privacy Policy, and plan documentation. MakeShipHappen does not mediate internal employment, team, access, or ownership disputes unless required by law or separate contract.

## Shared Workspaces and Local Data

Team administrators should understand that some product data may live locally on users' devices and may not be centrally visible, exportable, or deletable by MakeShipHappen or the administrator.
