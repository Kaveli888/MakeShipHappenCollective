# Privacy Policy Attorney Review Brief

Purpose: hand this to a SaaS/privacy attorney to draft a product-accurate Privacy Policy.

## Products Covered

Website/account, ShipMind, ShipSpace, ShipTalk, ShipCode, ShipWatch, ShipTranscribe, Ship AOS, Ship Memory, MCP servers, prompt libraries, community, merch.

## Data Classes To Cover

1. Account email/profile.
2. Password/auth handled by Supabase.
3. Subscription/billing metadata.
4. Payment card data handled by Stripe.
5. Shipping/merch data handled by Printful.
6. Usage events.
7. Error reports.
8. Prompts and AI conversations.
9. Source code context.
10. Documents/PDFs/web/YouTube sources.
11. Audio recordings.
12. Transcripts.
13. Voice input.
14. Screenshots.
15. OCR text.
16. Clipboard content.
17. App/window/browser URL context.
18. Local memory notes.
19. API keys and provider credentials.
20. Local app storage and local SQLite/WebKit/localStorage data.

## Required Disclosures

1. Some products are local-first, but not every feature is local-only.
2. Cloud providers may receive prompts, audio, transcripts, excerpts, code, or metadata depending on configuration.
3. BYO provider usage is governed by provider terms.
4. Local-only data may not be visible to or deletable by MakeShipHappen.
5. Account deletion does not automatically delete local device data.
6. Billing/tax/fraud records may be retained.
7. Recording/capture features may collect third-party personal data.
8. Users are responsible for consent and authority when recording or monitoring.
9. Subprocessors vary by product and feature.
10. Users can request access/export/deletion through privacy contact.

## Rights Workflow To Describe

1. Access.
2. Export.
3. Correction.
4. Deletion.
5. Objection/restriction where applicable.
6. California privacy rights.
7. EU/UK rights if serving those users.
8. Appeal process if required.

## Policy Contradictions To Avoid

1. Do not say "we never see AI conversations" unless true for all hosted routes.
2. Do not say "no recordings are uploaded" unless cloud transcription is excluded or clearly conditional.
3. Do not say "no telemetry" if usage/error events exist.
4. Do not promise deletion/export without operational process.
5. Do not omit Printful if merch is live.
6. Do not imply HIPAA/FERPA/GDPR certification unless actually supported by contract and controls.
