# Terms of Service Attorney Review Brief

Purpose: hand this to a SaaS/privacy/AI attorney to draft or revise enforceable Terms.

## Business Context

MakeShipHappen is currently a personal/founder-operated AI software ecosystem transitioning into a formal business. It includes web subscriptions, desktop apps, local-first knowledge tools, voice transcription, AI coding agents, terminal/workspace tools, CLI distribution, local memory, MCP integrations, community, and merch.

Current commercial baseline:

1. Current official price: $50/month.
2. Current refund policy: no refunds.
3. Current downloadable products: ShipMind, ShipSpace, ShipTalk, and ShipCode.
4. Current release posture: frequent updates, including weekly and sometimes daily updates.
5. Future planned offer: $1,200 lifetime access option, not to be published without finalized terms.

## Required Terms Outcomes

1. Make the contract enforceable through clear acceptance at signup and checkout.
2. Cap liability.
3. Allocate responsibility for AI outputs and generated code to users.
4. Allocate recording/capture compliance to users.
5. Allocate local device, local data, and BYO provider responsibility to users.
6. Protect platform IP.
7. Protect against misuse of AI/agent/terminal/capture tools.
8. Reconcile pricing, plans, refunds, cancellation, and product status.
9. Cover beta, preview, experimental, and coming-soon features.
10. Cover third-party services and provider terms.

## Specific Issues Attorney Must Resolve

1. Pricing cleanup: replace legacy Free/Pro/Team language with current $50/month baseline unless intentionally retained for future.
2. Refund cleanup: unify all documents around no refunds; make pre-purchase disclosure conspicuous.
3. Minimum age mismatch: choose one policy.
4. Local-first claims vs cloud-provider functionality.
5. Recording consent in California and other all-party consent jurisdictions.
6. Agent-caused local file/code damage.
7. Team member/admin responsibility.
8. BYO AI provider terms and costs.
9. OSS/license and bundled binary distribution.
10. Consumer subscription and click-to-cancel compliance.

## No-Refund Clause Requirements

The attorney should draft no-refund language that is:

1. Clear before purchase.
2. Consistent across Terms, checkout, pricing, support, and FAQs.
3. Compatible with applicable consumer law.
4. Clear about subscription cancellation versus refund.
5. Clear that cancellation stops future renewals but does not refund already-paid periods unless required by law.
6. Clear for future lifetime access, if offered.

## Lifetime Access Clause Requirements

If the $1,200 lifetime option is launched, define:

1. What "lifetime" means: product lifetime, account lifetime, founder/company lifetime, or another definition.
2. Which products are included.
3. Whether future products are included.
4. Whether updates are included.
5. Whether support is included.
6. Whether transfer/resale is allowed.
7. What happens if a product is discontinued.
8. Whether lifetime access is refundable. Current founder decision: no refunds.
