# Recording, Capture, and Transcription Responsibility Policy

Status: Public-facing draft for attorney review.

MakeShipHappen products may support voice input, audio transcription, meeting transcription, screen capture, OCR, clipboard capture, browser/window context, system audio capture, and activity memory.

## User Consent Responsibility

Users are responsible for obtaining all required consent, authorization, and notices before recording, transcribing, monitoring, capturing, storing, or analyzing:

1. Voices.
2. Meetings.
3. Calls.
4. Screens.
5. Browser URLs.
6. Window titles.
7. Documents.
8. Clipboard content.
9. Customer, employee, student, patient, client, or bystander information.

## No Covert Surveillance

Users may not use MakeShipHappen products for covert surveillance, unauthorized employee monitoring, stalking, nonconsensual recording, hidden capture, or collection of data from people who have not received legally required notice.

## Sensitive Contexts

Extra caution is required for:

1. Two-party/all-party consent jurisdictions.
2. Workplaces.
3. Schools.
4. Healthcare settings.
5. Legal/client matters.
6. Financial accounts.
7. Password managers.
8. Customer support calls.
9. Children's data.
10. Confidential third-party material.

## Local and Cloud Modes

Some features may process data locally, while others may send audio, transcripts, prompts, excerpts, screenshots, summaries, or context to third-party providers depending on configuration and provider selection. Users must review product documentation and provider settings before using capture features in sensitive contexts.

## User Controls

Users should:

1. Disable capture when not needed.
2. Configure blocked apps and exclusions where available.
3. Avoid capturing password managers, banking, healthcare, legal, or confidential third-party screens.
4. Delete local capture data when no longer needed.
5. Use local-only modes where confidentiality is required.
6. Notify meeting participants before recording or transcription.
