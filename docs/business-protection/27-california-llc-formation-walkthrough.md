# California LLC Formation Walkthrough

Purpose: give Jake a practical founder checklist for moving MakeShipHappen from personal project to business entity without changing product functionality.

This is an operational checklist, not legal or tax advice. Final decisions should be reviewed by a California business attorney and CPA.

Official sources checked June 7, 2026:

- California Secretary of State LLC forms/fees: https://www.sos.ca.gov/business-programs/cannabizfile/cannabis-forms-and-fees/limited-liability-companies-llc-california
- California Secretary of State business entity FAQ: https://www.sos.ca.gov/business-programs/business-entities/faqs
- California Franchise Tax Board 2026 Form 3522 instructions: https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2026/2026-3522.pdf

## Current State Baseline

## Step 1 - Name And Public Record Prep

Choose the exact legal entity name before filing.

Recommended candidate:

> MakeShipHappen LLC

Check name availability in California BizFile before filing.

Decide whether to use:

- Home address: lowest cost, but public-record exposure.
- Registered agent/commercial mailbox: better privacy and cleaner business presentation.

Important: California states that an individual agent's physical street address is public record. Treat this as a privacy decision before filing.

## Step 2 - File Articles Of Organization

File Articles of Organization online through California BizFile.

Official state fee page currently lists:

- Formation - Articles of Organization: $70.
- Online filing.

Keep:

- Filed Articles of Organization.
- State file number.
- Filing receipt.
- Registered agent information.

## Step 3 - File Statement Of Information

The California Secretary of State currently states LLC Statement of Information is:

- Due within 90 days of initial registration.
- Due every two years thereafter.
- Current listed fee: $20.

Put the initial 90-day deadline and next biennial filing window on the business calendar immediately after formation.

## Step 4 - Federal EIN

Apply for an EIN directly with the IRS after the LLC is formed.

Use the EIN for:

- Business bank account.
- Stripe/tax profile.
- Payment processor records.
- Vendor accounts.
- Insurance applications.
- Attorney/CPA intake.

Do not use paid EIN services unless there is a specific reason.

## Step 5 - Operating Agreement

Create a single-member LLC operating agreement.

Include:

- Jake as sole member unless ownership changes.
- Authority to operate software, AI products, websites, subscriptions, and digital goods.
- Separation of business and personal finances.
- IP contribution/assignment from founder to LLC if attorney approves.
- Manager authority.
- Records, accounting, and dissolution rules.

Attorney review is recommended because the software IP is the core business asset.

## Step 6 - California Tax Setup

The Franchise Tax Board 2026 Form 3522 instructions show the annual LLC tax due by the 15th day of the 4th month after the beginning of the taxable year, with calendar-year payment listed as April 15, 2026.

Founder action:

- Ask CPA to confirm first-year and ongoing California LLC tax timing.
- Add annual tax reminders.
- Confirm whether any LLC gross receipts fee applies as revenue grows.
- Confirm federal tax election strategy.

## Step 7 - Business Bank And Payments

Open a business bank account after EIN.

Move business activity to the LLC:

- Stripe account or Stripe business profile.
- Domain/email billing.
- Hosting.
- Software tools.
- Contractor payments.
- Insurance payments.

Do not mix personal and business revenue once the LLC account is active.

## Step 8 - Contract And Policy Alignment

Update governance docs and public-facing legal docs to use the legal entity name once formed:

- Terms of Service.
- Privacy Policy.
- Refund/no-refund policy.
- Security contact.
- Billing contact.
- Stripe descriptor/customer receipts.
- Website footer.
- Invoices/receipts.

## Step 9 - Records Binder

Create a folder for:

- Articles of Organization.
- Statement of Information.
- EIN letter.
- Operating Agreement.
- FTB tax confirmations.
- Stripe account records.
- Business bank records.
- Insurance policies.
- Attorney/CPA correspondence.
- Terms/Privacy versions by date.

## Founder Caution List

- Do not sell lifetime access until transferability, update rights, support scope, cancellation, survivability, and no-refund terms are attorney-reviewed.
- Do not claim HIPAA, FERPA, SOC 2, GDPR certification, or enterprise security compliance unless separately reviewed and supported.
- Do not use personal bank accounts for business revenue after the LLC is operating.
- Do not ignore California Statement of Information deadlines.
- Do not treat the LLC as liability protection if business and personal records are mixed.
