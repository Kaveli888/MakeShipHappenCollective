## Locked Founder Decisions

These decisions are the current operating baseline and should be reflected consistently in Terms, Privacy, pricing copy, checkout, product pages, support replies, and onboarding:

1. Current operating posture: personal/founder-operated project transitioning into a formal business.
2. Current official price: $50/month.
3. Future planned offer: $1,200 lifetime access option.
4. Refund policy: no refunds; be upfront before purchase that users pay for access as provided.
5. Current public downloadable products: ShipMind, ShipSpace, ShipTalk, and ShipCode.
6. Release posture: frequent updates, including weekly and sometimes daily updates.
7. Product posture: honest and transparent about what exists now, what is still evolving, and what users receive.
8. Entity posture: do not form the LLC immediately. Revisit entity formation once the website reliably clears approximately $5,000-$8,000/month or before any higher-risk B2B, team, enterprise, contractor, investor, or lifetime-access expansion.

1. Product status.
2. Privacy matrix.
3. Claims register.
4. Subprocessor register.
5. Terms/Privacy impact.
6. Release governance checklist.
