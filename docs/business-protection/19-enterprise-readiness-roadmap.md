# Enterprise Readiness Roadmap

Purpose: long-term governance plan for selling MakeShipHappen to teams, businesses, and regulated buyers without overpromising.

## Stage 1 — Founder-Grade Controls

1. Entity and banking separation.
2. Terms/Privacy/AUP published.
3. Product status matrix maintained.
4. Subprocessor list maintained.
5. Security contact active.
6. Incident plan active.
7. Claims register active.
8. Cyber + Tech E&O quotes requested.

## Stage 2 — Business-Grade Controls

1. Vendor risk reviews.
2. Annual legal/privacy review.
3. Release governance checklist for every launch.
4. Data retention schedule enforced operationally.
5. Deletion/export SOP tested quarterly.
6. Support/dispute playbook.
7. Team/admin addendum.
8. OSS notices included in distributions.

## Stage 3 — Enterprise-Grade Controls

1. Data Processing Addendum.
2. Security Addendum.
3. AI Governance Addendum.
4. Enterprise support terms.
5. Trust center.
6. SOC 2 readiness gap assessment.
7. Access control policy.
8. Change management policy.
9. Business continuity plan.
10. Vendor DPA archive.
11. Annual penetration/security assessment.
12. Privacy impact assessments for capture/audio/surveillance features.

## Enterprise Positioning Rule

Do not claim HIPAA, FERPA, SOC 2, GDPR compliance, or enterprise certification until the required contracts, controls, evidence, and attorney-reviewed language exist.
