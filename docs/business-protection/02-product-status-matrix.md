# Product Status Matrix

Purpose: prevent users from reasonably believing that every advertised product and feature is fully available, production-ready, supported, or enterprise-grade.

## Current Public Offer

Current official offer:

- $50/month.
- No refunds.
- Access to the currently downloadable MakeShipHappen products.
- Current downloadable products: ShipMind, ShipSpace, ShipTalk, and ShipCode.
- Updates are frequent and may ship weekly or daily.
- Product functionality is real and available, but the ecosystem is actively evolving.

Future planned offer:

- $1,200 lifetime access option.
- This should not be advertised as available until the purchase terms, refund/no-refund language, transferability, included products, update rights, and support expectations are attorney-reviewed.

Status definitions:

- Live: available to users and commercially supportable.
- Beta: usable, but may change and should carry beta disclaimers.
- Preview: shown publicly, not relied on for paid commitments.
- Internal: used by founder/team, not a public product promise.
- Planned: roadmap item, not yet shippable.
- Experimental: high-risk or non-standard behavior; user assumes extra responsibility.

## Required Status Language

Use one of these labels on public materials:

- "Available now"
- "Beta access"
- "Preview"
- "Coming soon"
- "Internal tool"
- "Experimental feature"

Avoid mixed labels like "Live", "Soon", "Download", and "Coming soon" on the same product unless the difference is platform-specific and explicitly stated.

## Required Pricing/Access Language

Use language like:

"MakeShipHappen access is currently $50/month and includes access to the current downloadable products: ShipMind, ShipSpace, ShipTalk, and ShipCode. These products are actively evolving, with frequent updates. There are no refunds, so review the product status and included access before purchasing."

Do not use:

- "Free/Pro/Team" unless that pricing model is intentionally reintroduced.
- "7-day refund" or "14-day refund."
- "Lifetime" until the $1,200 lifetime terms are finalized.
- "Enterprise-ready" or regulated-compliance language unless separately contracted and reviewed.
