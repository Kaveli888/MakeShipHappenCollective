# Acceptable Use Policy

Status: Public-facing draft for attorney review.

MakeShipHappen products are powerful AI, automation, transcription, local-file, and developer tools. Users may only use them lawfully, responsibly, and with appropriate authorization.

## Prohibited Uses

Users may not use MakeShipHappen products to:

1. Violate laws, regulations, contracts, platform terms, or third-party rights.
2. Create, distribute, or assist malware, credential theft, phishing, bot abuse, unauthorized access, or evasion.
3. Access, modify, damage, exfiltrate, or interfere with systems, accounts, files, networks, or repositories without authorization.
4. Record, transcribe, monitor, capture, or surveil people without required consent or legal authority.
5. Capture workplace, customer, student, patient, client, or bystander data in violation of applicable policies or law.
6. Upload, ingest, scrape, copy, or process copyrighted, confidential, privileged, private, or third-party material without sufficient rights.
7. Use generated outputs as professional legal, medical, financial, tax, employment, safety, or compliance advice without qualified human review.
8. Use the products in safety-critical, emergency, weapons, critical infrastructure, child-safety, medical, law-enforcement, or regulated high-risk decision systems without a separate written agreement.
9. Harass, stalk, defame, impersonate, discriminate, or generate abusive content.
10. Resell, sublicense, bypass metering, abuse promotions, overload infrastructure, or interfere with other users.
11. Reverse engineer proprietary platform components except where law expressly allows.
12. Misrepresent MakeShipHappen outputs as independently verified, certified, professional, or guaranteed.

## User Representations

By using the products, the user represents that:

1. They have authority to process the files, code, recordings, transcripts, sources, and other materials they provide.
2. They will obtain all required consents for recording, transcription, screen capture, clipboard capture, OCR, and monitoring.
3. They will review, test, and verify AI outputs before relying on them.
4. They are responsible for activity under their account, local machine, API keys, provider accounts, team workspaces, and repositories.
5. They will comply with third-party provider terms, including AI providers, payment providers, app stores, code hosts, and source platforms.

## Enforcement

MakeShipHappen may suspend or terminate access, refuse support, revoke promotional access, block downloads, or report unlawful conduct where reasonably necessary to protect users, the platform, third parties, or the company.
