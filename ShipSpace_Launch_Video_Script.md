# ShipSpace — Launch Video Script (v2)

**Format:** Founder to camera (you, Jake) · **Runtime:** ~3:00 · **Tone:** Empowered & inspirational, with a persuasion spine · **Ends on:** Website sign-up

> v2 weaves in your Jim Rohn quotes + the 7-step persuasion blueprint (paradigm hook → two-types split → relief trigger → curiosity → proof → value → future pacing → sign-off) — but keeps your authentic, non-salesy first-impression vibe. The tactic for each beat is marked in *[brackets]* so you can see the strategy. Those brackets are notes, not spoken.

## THE SCRIPT (what you say)

**[0:00 — HOOK / PARADIGM SHIFT. Look into the lens. One beat of silence first.]**
*[Tactic: Paradigm-shift hook]*

Jim Rohn said it best: **"You can have more than you've got — because you can become more than you are."**

Read that again. Everything you want is on the other side of who you're willing to become.

**[0:16 — THE TWO-TYPES SPLIT. Pick up energy.]**
*[Tactic: Divide the audience — force self-identification]*

Right now there are two kinds of builders in the world.

One is still doing it the old way — grinding alone, doing everything by hand, watching the world move faster than they can keep up.

The other one figured out how to build *with* AI. And they're shipping things in days that used to take teams months.

The only difference between them isn't talent. It isn't money. It's the tools they decided to pick up.

**[0:42 — RELIEF TRIGGER. Slow down, sincere. This builds trust.]**
*[Tactic: Shift the blame — "it's not your fault"]*

And if you've been working harder than everyone you know and still feel stuck — hear me: your effort was never the problem.

You were just handed outdated methods. Nobody showed you the new way. That's not a flaw in you. That's a gap in what you were taught — and we're about to close it.

**[1:05 — THE THREAT + CURIOSITY GAP. Quieter, a little urgent.]**
*[Tactic: Cost of inaction + open a loop]*

Because here's the truth nobody's saying out loud: success didn't disappear. It just took on a new face.

The builders who adapt to AI right now are about to pull so far ahead that catching up later won't be an option. And what I'm about to show you — most people aren't teaching it yet.

**[1:25 — THE REVEAL / AUTHORITY. Confident. This is who you are.]**
*[Tactic: Authority + "show don't tell"]*

I'm Jake, and I built ShipSpace with my team at MSH.Tech — built *by* AI agents, *for* the builders coming next.

ShipSpace is an AI agent development environment. Run Claude, Codex, Gemini, Cursor — your favorite agents — side by side in one workspace. Not one at a time. *Together.* Up to sixteen agents working in parallel, like a real engineering team that never sleeps.

**[1:50 — VALUE STACK. Let each one land as a solution, not a feature.]**
*[Tactic: Features framed as relief from the pain above]*

No more doing it all yourself. Ship Agent orchestrates your whole AI workforce for you.

No more jumping between ten apps. A built-in browser, a full IDE, and an integrated terminal — all in one place, connected straight to GitHub.

Drag a terminal right into an agent's workflow. Collaborate with your agents in real time. And spin up a whole multi-agent workspace in under a second.

Everything you need to build — finally in one place.

**[2:18 — THE HEART. This is your emotional core. Slow way down. Mean it.]**
*[Tactic: Jim Rohn payoff + the "nothing into something" promise]*

But I don't want you to remember the features. I want you to remember this.

If you have nothing right now — no team, no code, no permission — that is not a disadvantage. That's a starting line.

Jim Rohn also said: **"Work hard at your job and you can make a living. Work hard on yourself and you can make a fortune."** ShipSpace is where you do both — you build the thing *and* you become the builder who can.

You can take an idea and turn it into a business. Keep it private — it's yours. Nothing, into something.

**[2:48 — FUTURE PACING. Warm, vivid. Let them see it.]**
*[Tactic: Picture-this visualization]*

So picture this. Six months from now. The idea you can't stop thinking about isn't an idea anymore — it's real. It's running. People are using it. And you built it with a team of agents that didn't exist until you decided to start.

**[3:05 — CTA + SIGN-OFF. Direct eye contact. Warm, not salesy.]**
*[Tactic: Clear instruction + memorable, brand-specific close]*

That version of you is one decision away. Go to ShipSpace and sign up — start building today.

Built by agents. Built for builders. I'll see you in there.

Ship faster. 🚀

**[End.]**

## DELIVERY NOTES

**Pacing.** ~3:00 at a natural pace. This is inspirational *with* a persuasion spine — never let it tip into infomercial. The trust comes from sincerity, especially in the Relief Trigger (0:42) and the Heart (2:18). Slow down there.

**Energy map.** Quiet hook → lift on the two-types split → drop into the relief ("it's not your fault") → urgency on the threat → confident reveal → punchy value stack → drop ALL the way down for the heart → rise into future pacing → land the CTA. The contrast is what makes a single talking-head shot feel cinematic.

**The two lines to nail:**
1. *"Your effort was never the problem."* — this is your trust-builder. Say it like you mean it personally.
2. *"That is not a disadvantage. That's a starting line."* — your quotable moment.

**Eyes & setting.** Look into the lens, treat it like one person. Keep the background real — your actual workspace beats a studio for a first impression.
