Rules:

Every section must be filled. If you genuinely have nothing, write "no signal yet" — never pad.
Keep every bullet under 15 words. This is a scan, not a read.
"MY MOVES THIS WEEK" must be concrete actions, not vague directions. "Ship pricing page" not "improve pricing."
"MY ANGLE" under weaknesses must be a real differentiator I can actually execute on.
Threat level reasoning: 🔴 if they're directly in my lane and gaining, 🟡 if adjacent or stalled, 🟢 if drifting away or stuck.
Trajectory: judge based on last 90 days of activity, not all-time.
If sources span less than 30 days, mark trajectory as "?" — not enough data.
